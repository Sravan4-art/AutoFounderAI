---

## 1. Market Definition
| Category | What to Capture |
|----------|-----------------|
| **Geographic Scope** | City, metro, county, or specific neighborhood? |
| **Segment** | Fine dining, casual, fast‑casual, ethnic, farm‑to‑table, etc. |
| **Customer Demographics** | Age, income, household size, lifestyle. |
| **Customer Psychographics** | Food values (e.g., health‑conscious, comfort food, culinary adventurer). |
| **Revenue & Volume Metrics** | Avg. spend per visit, frequency, peak periods. |

### Quick Check‑List
- Is the market saturated with similar concepts?  
- Are there underserved niches (e.g., vegan, keto, low‑carb)?  
- What are the most common dining‑out motives in the area (e.g., work lunches, date nights, family gatherings)?

---

## 2. Competitive Landscape (Even if you list no direct competitors, consider indirect competition)
| Source | What to Look For |
|--------|-----------------|
| **Online Review Sites** | TripAdvisor, Yelp, Google Reviews, OpenTable. |
| **Delivery Apps** | DoorDash, Uber Eats, Grubhub – see what’s topping the charts. |
| **Local Food Media** | Food blogs, local newspapers, Instagram food influencers. |
| **Economic Data** | Restaurant density per 10,000 residents from city stats. |

**Indirect Competitors**  
- Coffee shops for lunch, grocery‑store prepared meals, catering services, food‑truck parks, meal‑prep subscription boxes.

---

## 3. Consumer Trends
| Trend | Why It Matters |
|-------|----------------|
| **Health & Wellness** | Demand for plant‑based, gluten‑free, low‑calorie options. |
| **Sustainability** | Preference for locally sourced, seasonal menus. |
| **Experience Over Price** | Willingness to pay more for unique ambiance, themed nights, chef‑table experiences. |
| **Digital Ordering** | Mobile menus, contactless payments, loyalty apps. |
| **Delivery & Take‑out** | Continued growth, especially post‑COVID. |

---

## 4. Pricing & Positioning
1. **Cost Structure Analysis**  
   - Food cost %, labor %, overhead, rent.  
   - Compare to industry averages (food 28–32 %, labor 25–30 %, overhead 15–20 %).
2. **Menu Mix**  
   - Value‑to‑Premium ratio.  
   - “Signature dishes” that can be marketed as unique selling points.
3. **Revenue Streams**  
   - Dine‑in vs. take‑out vs. delivery vs. catering.  
   - Events (wine tastings, cooking classes).

## 5. SWOT Snapshot (Quick Brainstorm)

| Strengths | Weaknesses | Opportunities | Threats |
|-----------|------------|----------------|---------|

## 6. Regulatory & Operational Checks
- **Health & Safety**: Zoning, permits, sanitation standards.  
- **Labor Law**: Minimum wage, tipped‑employee regulations.  
- **Supply Chain**: Reliable suppliers, seasonal variability, local sourcing contracts.  
- **Technology**: POS system integration, data privacy for customer loyalty programs.

## 7. Data Sources to Deepen the Analysis
1. **U.S. Census Bureau** – demographic and economic data.

3. **Local Chamber of Commerce** – restaurant counts, foot traffic stats.  
4. **NPD Group** – consumer food trend reports.  
5. **Google Trends** – search interest for specific cuisines or keywords.

COMPETITOR ANALYSIS

**Top Competitors**  
- Bistro & Co. (fine‑dining chain)  
- The Daily Grill (fast‑casual steakhouse)  
- Farmhouse Kitchen (farm‑to‑table concept)  

**Strengths**  
- **Bistro & Co.** – upscale ambience, loyal clientele, strong brand.  
- **The Daily Grill** – quick service, high volume, low price point.  
- **Farmhouse Kitchen** – organic menu, local sourcing, eco‑brand.  

**Weaknesses**  
- **Bistro & Co.** – high cost, limited delivery, slow turnover.  
- **The Daily Grill** – menu sameness, weak ambience, lower‑quality ingredients.  
- **Farmhouse Kitchen** – premium prices, narrow menu, slow service.  

**Market Gaps**  
- Authentic regional cuisine at mid‑price.  
- Hybrid dining: in‑house experience + fast, quality delivery.  
- Sustainable menu without premium markup.  

**How to Beat Them**  
1. Offer signature regional dishes, mid‑price, fast delivery.  
2. Upgrade kitchen tech for speed & consistency.  
3. Build loyalty program & partner with local producers.  
4. Highlight transparent sourcing & a compelling brand story.

--------------------------------

SWOT ANALYSIS

**Strengths**  
- Fresh, locally sourced ingredients give a competitive edge.  
- Flexible menu allows rapid adaptation to trends.  
- Strong focus on customer experience (personalized service, ambiance).  
- Efficient kitchen layout designed for high turnover.  

**Weaknesses**  
- Limited brand recognition in a crowded market.  
- Higher operating costs due to quality sourcing.  
- Initial marketing budget constrained.  

**Opportunities**  
- Growing demand for farm‑to‑table and sustainable dining.  
- Expansion into delivery/ghost‑kitchen models to reach wider audience.  
- Partnerships with local farms, artisans, and food‑tech platforms.  
- Capitalizing on health‑conscious and plant‑based trends.  

**Threats**  
- Established chains with loyal followings.  
- Rising food and labor costs.  
- Market saturation in key segments (fine dining, fast‑casual).  
- Economic downturns affecting discretionary spending.
