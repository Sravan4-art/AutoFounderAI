**How to Beat Them**

SWOT ANALYSIS

**Strengths**  
- Prime urban location with high foot traffic and easy parking.  
- Fresh, locally sourced menu that differentiates us from generic chains.  
- Experienced kitchen staff and streamlined prep that keeps wait times low.  
- Growing social‑media presence that attracts millennial diners.  

**Weaknesses**  
- Limited brand recognition in a crowded market.  
- Higher food and labor costs due to premium ingredients.  
- Seasonal menu requires frequent ingredient sourcing, adding complexity.  
- Small dining space limits table turnover during peak hours.  

**Opportunities**  
- Partner with on‑demand delivery apps to reach non‑walk‑in customers.  
- Host themed pop‑ups and cooking classes to build community buzz.  
- Leverage local farms for co‑branding and “farm‑to‑table” storytelling.  
- Introduce a loyalty program tied to mobile ordering.  

**Threats**  
- Aggressive price wars from fast‑food and casual competitors.  
- Rising rent and supply‑chain volatility pushing operating costs upward.  
- Regulatory changes on health‑safety or labor that could increase overhead.  
- Negative reviews or viral social‑media backlash from a single incident.
